# Glow Halal — 30-Day Marketing & Branding Plan (Aug 2026)

**Date:** 11 August 2026. Produced by brand + growth + planning agents against the
live store state. Budget: **Rs 6,000-9,500 total**, zero ads this month, 1-2 hrs/day.

## 1. Brand priorities

### Lock a one-page brand kit from the existing gold/dark logo (Day 1-2)
- **Why:** Consistency is what separates a brand from a random COD page. Right now every future asset (FB posts, insert cards, WhatsApp images) risks looking different. A written kit means every touchpoint matches from day one, even when made in 10-minute bursts.
- **How:** 1) Open the logo in any image tool and eyedropper the exact hex codes: the gold, the dark base, plus pick one neutral cream (e.g. #F5EFE0-ish) for backgrounds — write all three down. 2) Pick exactly two free Google fonts and never deviate: a serif for headings (Marcellus or Cormorant Garamond reads 'premium herbal') + Inter or Poppins for body; add Noto Nastaliq Urdu only if Urdu-script assets come later. 3) In Canva Free, create a Glow Halal folder with 4 master templates: FB/IG square post, story/status, WhatsApp order-image, blog/offer banner — dark background, gold accents, cream text panels. 4) Save the kit as one page (colors, fonts, logo clear-space rule: never stretch, never on busy photos, minimum size ~24px) and reuse it for everything.
- **Cost:** Rs 0, ~2 hours one time

### Shoot a real product photo set with daylight only (Week 1)
- **Why:** Pakistani buyers judge COD trustworthiness by photos first. Supplier stock images scream reseller; original photos on a consistent backdrop scream brand. These photos feed the website, Merchant Center feed, FB page, and future Daraz store all at once.
- **How:** 1) Buy props once from any Sunday bazaar/Daraz: a plain beige/off-white chador or craft paper backdrop, a wooden tray or slab, small bowls of dried herbs (kalonji, dried rose, cloves) as styling. 2) Shoot near a window between 10am-2pm, phone camera, no flash: per SKU capture 6 shots — front label straight-on, 45-degree hero, in-hand for scale, texture drop on skin/back of hand, flat-lay with herbs, and the sealed parcel ready to ship. 3) Edit only brightness/warmth in Snapseed (free); no heavy filters so all photos match. 4) Repeat the identical setup for every future SKU (soap, cream) so the catalog looks like one family. 5) Replace current basic images on the site and use the parcel shot in WhatsApp confirmations.
- **Cost:** Rs 1,500-3,000 one-time props; 3-4 hours

### Unify the handle and claim Instagram + TikTok + Google Business Profile this week
- **Why:** glowhalalpk must be identical everywhere before someone else takes it. A Google Business Profile with the 0301 2973886 number is the single biggest free trust signal for a Pakistani buyer who googles the brand before paying COD, and it feeds the knowledge panel next to the SEO work already done.
- **How:** 1) Register @glowhalalpk on Instagram and TikTok today even if posting starts later — matching handle, same logo avatar, same one-line bio ('Halal beauty & personal care | Karachi | COD all Pakistan | WhatsApp 0301 2973886'). 2) Create Google Business Profile as a service-area/online retailer (no public address needed — hide address, set service area Pakistan), add logo, the new product photos, website link, phone. - **Cost:** Rs 0, ~2 hours plus verification wait

### Write the About page founder story (Week 1-2)
- **Why:** Halal-conscious buyers purchase from people, not pages. A named human, a why, and a Karachi location convert skeptics better than any discount. It is also the page that makes the multi-category ambition legible: one family of halal personal-care products, not one oil.
- **How:** Structure (300-400 words, EN with Roman Urdu phrases woven in): 1) The spark — why the owner started with traditional herbal formulations (personal/family use of Lookman-e-Hayat style oils). 2) The promise — 'hum sirf wohi bechte hain jo hum khud apne ghar mein use karein': honest ingredients, honest wording, no exaggerated claims, DRAP-listed only for supplements. 3) What Glow Halal is becoming — oils today, sidr soap, face cream, hair oil next. 4) A real signature: owner first name, 'Karachi, Pakistan', and the WhatsApp number. Add one photo: the owner's hands packing a parcel (face optional). Explicitly include the honesty line 'hum medical claims nahi karte — ye cosmetics hain, dawai nahi' — that sentence itself builds more trust than any claim could.
- **Cost:** Rs 0, ~2 hours

### Print the packaging insert card + brand sticker; design the unboxing (Week 2-3)
- **Why:** Unboxing is the only physical brand moment a COD customer gets, and it is what earns the second order and the family referral. A Rs 8-per-parcel insert converts a one-time buyer into a review and a repeat.

### Stand up the review-collection loop (Week 3-4)
- **Why:** Zero-fake-review policy means real reviews are the only social proof allowed — so a system must manufacture the ASK, not the review. 10 genuine WhatsApp screenshots with consent beat 100 fabricated stars, and they are legally and religiously clean.
- **How:** 1) Day 5-7 after delivery, send saved reply /feedback: 'Assalam o Alaikum! Glow Halal se — umeed hai aap ka order theek mila. 2 second mein bata dein: product kaisa laga? Target for the 30 days: every single delivered order gets the ask; even 5 real consented reviews transforms the product page.

## 2. Identity & voice

VISUAL: Keep the existing gold/dark logo as the anchor and build a strict 3-color system around it — deep charcoal/black base, the logo's gold for accents only (never large text blocks), and a warm cream for backgrounds/text panels; eyedropper the exact hex codes once and write them down. Fonts: one premium serif for headings (Marcellus or Cormorant Garamond) + Inter/Poppins for body, from Google Fonts, free forever. Photo style: natural daylight, beige/wood backdrops, dried herbs as props, no heavy filters — 'attar shop meets modern apothecary', not neon-discount-graphics. Gold-on-dark = premium halal positioning that justifies Rs 1,800-3,000 price points. VOICE: A respectful, honest elder-sibling tone — warm, unhurried, never desperate. Always 'aap', never 'tum'. Language split by channel: website product/SEO pages in clean English (search + Merchant Center); blogs already bilingual; WhatsApp and social captions Roman Urdu-first with natural English mixing ('Aap ka order confirm ho gaya hai — dispatch within 24 hours'), because that is how the customer actually types. Islamic courtesy naturally (Assalam o Alaikum, JazakAllah) but zero religious pressure-selling — halal is stated as identity and ingredient standard, never as guilt or a fatwa. Honesty is the brand's actual differentiator: say what the product does NOT do ('ye dawai nahi hai') as confidently as what it helps with ('daag halke karne mein madadgar'). TAGLINES (all DRAP-safe, no cure or certification claims): 1) 'Halal beauty, honestly made.' — already the working tagline; keep as the primary English lockup under the logo. 2) 'Khalis. Sada. Halal.' — three-word Roman Urdu stamp, perfect for stickers, packaging, and social watermarks. 3) 'Purani hikmat, naya bharosa.' (Old wisdom, new trust) — traditional-use framing that is inherently claim-free; strong for the About page and ad-ready later. Use 1 as the official tagline, 2 on packaging, 3 as the storytelling line — they don't compete.

## 4. Hard don'ts

- Never fake anything: no bought followers, no invented reviews or star ratings, no 'imported/certified' badges — one exposed fake in a Facebook mom group kills a COD brand permanently, and it violates the store's own compliance rules
- Never write 'halal certified' or issue halal/haram rulings — no certificate exists; 'halal' stays a brand identity and ingredient standard, nothing more
- Never let a cure claim slip in social captions or WhatsApp replies even when a customer begs for one ('bhai jal ka nishan guarantee jayega?') — reply with the honest 'madadgar' wording and the safety line; DRAP wording discipline applies to chat, not just the website
- Don't do the desperate-seller aesthetic: ALL-CAPS, 10 emojis, '70% OFF LAST DAY', fake urgency timers — it reads as scam page and destroys the premium gold/dark positioning; the brand's scarcity is honesty, not countdowns
- Don't scatter identity: one handle (glowhalalpk), one phone number, three colors, two fonts, everywhere, forever — no per-post font experiments, no new logo variants from a Rs 500 Fiverr gig
- Don't mix personal and business WhatsApp — no personal DP, no broadcast spam to past buyers more than ~2x/month, or the core sales channel gets blocked and reported
- Don't launch paid ads in these 30 days — with zero posts, zero reviews, and no GBP, ad clicks land on an unverifiable brand and burn money; trust assets first, ads after
- Don't chase new SKUs before the repeat-purchase loop works for the oil — soap/cream launches inherit the trust system (photos, inserts, templates); build the system once, not per product
- Don't over-religionize marketing — invoking faith as sales pressure ('musalman ho to yehi lo') reads as manipulation to the exact audience being served; courtesy yes, guilt never
- Don't go silent after delivery — an unanswered 'order kahan hai?' message is the #1 review killer in PK COD; the dispatch+tracking template must go out on every single order

## 8. WhatsApp playbook

WhatsApp is the cash register — everything else is a road that ends at 03012973886. CATALOG: list both SKUs with clear photos, prices (Rs 1,800 / Rs 3,000), DRAP-safe descriptions ('purane healed daag halke karne mein madadgar' — mirror site wording so the compliance gate applies here too), delivery time, and COD note. Set up quick replies for the 6 questions you'll get daily: price, delivery time/charges, how to use, 'kitne din mein asar hoga' (answer honestly: har skin alag, weeks not days), COD process, and the safety line for anyone mentioning a fresh burn (thanda paani + doctor, oil only on healed marks). Use labels religiously: New Lead / Ordered / Delivered / Repeat — this is your free CRM and your metrics source. STATUS (the underrated feed): post daily — packing clips, dispatch photos, restock news, consented customer feedback, Status-exclusive offers. Status viewers saved your number voluntarily, making it the highest-intent audience you own; goal is to convert every social follower into a Status viewer. BROADCAST RULES (the anti-spam covenant): broadcasts only reach people who saved your number AND you should still ask first — after a delivery, ask 'Hamari list mein add kar dein? Mahine mein sirf 2-3 messages — restock, offers, tips.' Hard limits: maximum 3-4 broadcasts per MONTH, every one must carry value (offer, restock, useful tip), never a bare 'buy now', never daily, and any 'remove kar dein' is honored instantly and politely. Never message a number that didn't contact you first, never buy lists — that path ends in a banned number and a dead business. Segment by label: repeat customers can hear about new launches (soap, cream) first; one-time buyers get at most the monthly offer. POST-ORDER FLOW (where reviews come from): (1) instant order confirmation with amount + expected days, (2) dispatch message with courier/tracking, (3) day-after-delivery check-in — 'Parcel theek mila? Koi sawal ho to poochein' — and in the same chat ask for honest feedback, permission to share it (recorded consent per UGC rule), and a Google review link once GBP is live. This one flow generates your proof pillar, your GBP reviews, and your repeat buyers simultaneously.

## 9. Weekly metrics (only these)

- 1. WhatsApp conversations started per week (any source — FB, TikTok, IG, GBP, site). The single number that predicts revenue; if it's flat, content isn't converting attention into intent. Count from chat list + labels, note the source when the customer mentions it ('TikTok pe dekha').
- 2. Conversation-to-order rate (%) — orders divided by new conversations. Pre-100-orders this measures your replies, honesty, and quick-reply quality; below ~20-25% means the closing script or price objection handling needs work, not more content.
- 3. Orders per week AND COD delivered-vs-refused split. Refusals are the silent killer of PK COD economics — track refusal % from week one; above ~15-20% means order confirmation messages need tightening (confirm on WhatsApp before dispatch, restate the amount).
- 4. Posts published vs planned (consistency score from the in-admin planner). For a solo owner the real risk is silent weeks; 10 of 12 planned posts shipped is a healthy week regardless of any post's performance.
- 5. Reach proxy: total video views + profile visits per platform (all follower-agnostic). At zero followers, follower count is vanity; views tell you which pillar/hook earns free distribution — double down on the top format each Sunday batch. Ignore likes as a decision input this month.
